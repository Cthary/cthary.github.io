## Overview

- 13 detachments and 27 datasheets. This is the first Codex to combine the Warhammer 40,000 and Horus Heresy ranges in one book. `[WHC]`
- Custodian Guard are now T7 with 5 W. **No Custodes squad exceeds 3 models** (before Leaders), and some have fewer. `[WHC]`
- Detachment structure `[WHC]`:
  - **Guardians of the Throne**: the only 3 DP detachment, with balanced bonuses for most units.
  - **6 detachments with Unique: Shield Host** (only one may be taken): Aquilan Shield, Dread Host, Emissaries Imperatus, Emperor's Chosen, Shadowkeepers, Solar Watch.
  - Freely combinable: Auric Champions, Honoured Companions, Lions of the Emperor, Might of the Moritoi, Grav-Assault Force, Null Maiden Vigil.
  - DP costs per WHC "Top Detachment Tips": Shadowkeepers, Honoured Companions, Null Maiden Vigil, Dread Host, Lions of the Emperor and Might of the Moritoi are **1 DP** each. WHC gives no figure for the others, probably also 1 DP (`unknown`). A 2,000-point game has 3 DP.
  - WHC recommendation: pick one Shield Host, then add further 1 DP detachments for your favourite units.
- Totals per Leak 2: 13 detachments, 29 enhancements/upgrades, 41 stratagem entries. `[Leak 2]`

## Army rules

### Martial Ka'tah `[WHC]`

Mechanic: units with Martial Ka'tah become **readied at the start of their own Command phase**. During the turn, when the WHEN condition is met, the unit can use **one** ka'tah, after which it is no longer readied. Ka'tahs are not limited to the Fight phase, and there are six to choose from. Some Characters and Stratagems can ready a unit again (e.g. Master of Ka'tahs, Superhuman Focus).

| Ka'tah | WHEN | TARGET | EFFECT |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservai** | Your Command phase. | One friendly readied unit. | Until the end of the turn: Being engaged/battle-shocked does not prevent your unit from being eligible to start an action. Starting an action does not prevent your unit from being eligible to shoot. |
| **Calistus** | Your Movement phase, when a friendly readied unit is selected to move. | That readied unit. | When your unit makes a normal/advance/fall-back move, your unit can move through all types of model. |
| **Salvus** | Your Shooting phase, when a friendly readied unit is selected to shoot. | That readied unit. | Your unit can ignore modifiers to your unit's: BS. Hit rolls and wound rolls. |
| **Dacatarai** | Start of the Fight phase. | One friendly readied unit. | Your unit's melee attacks that target an enemy unit (excluding MONSTER/VEHICLE units) can re-roll hit rolls of 1. |
| **Kaptaris** | Start of the Fight phase. | One friendly readied unit. | Melee attacks that target your unit have -1 to hit rolls. |
| **Rendax** | Fight phase, when a friendly readied unit is selected to fight. | One friendly readied unit. | Your unit's melee attacks have [LETHAL HITS: MONSTER/VEHICLE]. |

Favoured Ka'tah: each of the six Shield Host detachments has a favoured ka'tah with an **Additional Effect**. Other ka'tahs remain usable. `[WHC]`

### Aegis of the Emperor `[confirmed WHC]`

Friendly **ADEPTUS CUSTODES** units with this ability have **Feel No Pain 5+** against **mortal wounds**.

### Aquila Commander `[Leak]`

At the start of the battle round, if a model with this ability is your **WARLORD**, gain 1CP.

### Daughters of the Abyss `[confirmed WHC]`

- Friendly **ANATHEMA PSYKANA** units with this ability have **Feel No Pain 3+** against **psychic attacks**.
- Friendly **ANATHEMA PSYKANA** units with this ability have the following ability:
  - **Psychic Null (Aura):** Friendly **ADEPTUS CUSTODES** units within 6" of this unit have **Feel No Pain 5+** against **psychic attacks**.

### Anathema Psykana (army building) `[Leak]`

You can include **ANATHEMA PSYKANA** units in your army (pg 118-120), even though they do not have the **ADEPTUS CUSTODES** faction keyword. When mustering your army, unless otherwise stated:

- You cannot select **ANATHEMA PSYKANA** as your **army faction**.
- You cannot select an **ANATHEMA PSYKANA** model to be your **WARLORD**.

(Exception: the Null Maiden Vigil detachment allows an ANATHEMA PSYKANA CHARACTER as WARLORD.)

### Special Move Type: Shock Disembark Move `[Leak]`

| Field | Text |
|---|---|
| Maximum distance | 3" |
| ELIGIBLE IF | As stated in the rule allowing this **move type**, if all of the following apply to your unit: Embarked within a **TRANSPORT** model that is on the battlefield. Did not embark within that **TRANSPORT** this phase. |
| EFFECT | Your unit is set up as described in Set Up (Core Rules, 03.02). |
| WHILE MOVING | Set up each model in your unit wholly within the **set-up distance** of that **TRANSPORT**. |
| AFTER MOVING | Your unit is not **eligible to declare a charge** until the end of the turn. |

Used by: Coronus Grav-Carrier, Anathema Psykana Rhino (Assault Vehicle).

## Weaknesses according to GW `[WHC]`

From "How To Keep Your Custodians Standing Sentinel" (2026-09-29). Custodes are usually T7 or higher, so these are the main threats:

| Weapon ability | Why it is dangerous |
|---|---|
| Anti-X (especially Anti-Infantry) | Ignores the high Toughness when wounding. |
| Lance | Improves the wound roll on the charge (e.g. Sanguinary Guard, Black Templars vows). |
| Lethal Hits | Skips the wound roll entirely (Death Guard, Chaos Space Marines with Dark Pacts). |
| Melta | Bonus damage at half range against high-Wounds models (Eradicators, Fire Dragons, Retributors). |
| Devastating Wounds | No saves; especially dangerous against few models, only FNP 5+ against MW helps (World Eaters, Chaos Daemons). |
| Many high-damage attacks | Knights, Skarbrand, Ghazghkull. |

## Gaps and uncertainties

- **Unknown:** all enhancement points. Points for Sentinel Guard Sodality, Gyrfalcon Jetbike Sodality and Caladius Annihilator. Datasheets for the Ares Gunship and Orion Assault Dropship. DP costs of Solar Watch, Aquilan Shield, Emissaries Imperatus, Emperor's Chosen, Auric Champions and Grav-Assault Force. Leader attachments (which Character can lead which unit) are not on the photographed pages.
- **Rumour vs. WHC:** The rumour lists "Agamatus Custodians 260 / 390", but according to WHC Agamatus is **not in the Codex**. This weakens the reliability of the rumoured list.
- **Aquilon points:** The rumour gives 285 / 275 without a clear assignment to Gauntlets or Talons.
- **MFM v1.5** predates the Codex: different unit sizes, old detachments (Shield Host, Talons of the Emperor, old Auric Champions, old Lions, Solar Spearhead, Silent Hunters, Tharanatoi Hammerblow, old Might of the Moritoi, old Null Maiden Vigil) and Legends units. Its values were only used where the model count matches the Codex.
- **Photo quality:** all hard-to-read detachment passages from the first leak are resolved by Leak 2. Still hard to read on the datasheets: Coronus transport capacity, Galatus ranged Warblade, Knight-Centura profile, Witchseeker flamer range.
- **Auric Champions** has only 2 stratagems and 3 enhancements; Leak 2 confirms this (41 stratagem entries in total). The other detachments have 3 stratagems each, Guardians of the Throne has 6.
- **Leak 2** contains detachment rules only: no datasheets, no points, no DP costs and no general Martial Ka'tah rules.
- According to the WHC article, the balance updates of 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-01 contain no Custodes-specific changes.
